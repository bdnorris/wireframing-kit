card	card	One titled item with a short description, an optional updated date, and an optional list of tags. Use inside card-list, feature-grid, or another slot. Not as a page section on its own and not for pricing tiers.
card-list	section	A collection of cards with controls to sort by name or date and switch between a vertical list and a multi-column grid. Use inside tabs or sections to display a group of peer cards. Not for marketing feature highlights with a section heading (use feature-grid) or comparison tables.
form	form	A multi-control form with text inputs, textareas, a select dropdown, a radio group, and checkboxes. Use when a spec calls for creating or editing a record with varied field types. Not for a short contact form (use form-block), search bars, or inline table filters.
tabs	navigation	A heading plus labeled panels switched by tab controls. Use when a page needs two or more peer lists or views in one place. Not for site navigation, filters, or a single static section.
